## 👋 About Me

I'm **Mohammed Rokon Uddin**, a Senior iOS Engineer based in Dhaka with 10+ years of experience in Swift/SwiftUI, Clean Architecture and TCA. I've shipped large-scale production apps such as MyGP and work on on-device ML with Core ML and Vision.

**Why Data Engineering?** Reliable data pipelines are the foundation of good ML and product decisions. I'm combining Data Engineering with my mobile and ML background to build end-to-end systems.
